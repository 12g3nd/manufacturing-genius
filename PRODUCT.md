# Product: Manufacturing Genius

A statistical feature by Srihith Jarabana, written in first person. Readers play
the last round of a two-player contest, then follow a synthetic study of what a
winning score reveals about ability when players can choose risk. It will be
listed under Coverage on the Business page of jarabana.com, alongside an equity
research report and a published newspaper feature, and hosted on its own
subdomain. It doesn't have to match the portfolio's styling.

**Reader.** Mostly recruiters and finance people arriving from the portfolio.
They skim, play the game for a minute, and judge how the author thinks, so the
first screen has to show the idea and the game quickly. Quant-minded readers
should still be able to open the derivation and the full results ledger.

**Must stay true.** Exact derivations, synthetic study outputs and published
research stay clearly distinguished. Ability is separate from observed rank.
The model, seeds, saved results and source disclosures are fixed. Every number
shown is computed from the saved data. The page works as one offline HTML file
with no analysis server, and the exact payoff table and all evidence stay
readable without JavaScript.

**Would feel wrong.** Looking like a generic template (a stock Quarto or
newsletter article anyone could generate). Being so stylised or gimmicky that
the statistics stop looking serious.

**Visual constraints.** None pinned; earlier palette and font choices are open
to replacement.

The repository is 12g3nd/manufacturing-genius. The article is live at
manufacturinggenius.jarabana.com, served from the gh-pages branch; the author
handles hosting. The portfolio site is a separate repository.
